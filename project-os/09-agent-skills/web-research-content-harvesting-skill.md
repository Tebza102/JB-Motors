# Web Research Content Harvesting Skill

## Purpose
Gather relevant public web content and convert it into usable, traceable research notes.

## Required Capture
- source URL log
- page title
- extracted key points
- relevance to business objective
- content opportunities
- source quality notes
- outdated/unverified claim warnings

## Safety Rules
- do not copy content directly
- do not ignore source ownership
- do not present uncited claims as verified
- do not scrape private/restricted content
- do not ignore access restrictions

## Copyright-Safe Transformation
- summarize in original wording
- attribute source context
- separate facts from interpretation
- mark uncertain claims clearly

## Required Output
1. source log
2. distilled findings
3. opportunity map
4. risk and verification notes

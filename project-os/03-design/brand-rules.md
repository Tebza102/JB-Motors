# Brand Rules

## Brand Positioning
JB Motors is a professional, technically competent, trustworthy, practical, and established automotive workshop serving Ratanda and Heidelberg. The platform should feel commissioned and owned by JB Motors, not reskinned from a generic ERP.

## Visual Direction
Refined industrial-business design: charcoal navigation, clean white and warm-grey work surfaces, metallic-grey separators, compact grids, high-density tables, restrained shadows, and warm orange actions.

## Colours
- Primary charcoal: `#202224`
- Deep ink: `#161718`
- Warm orange reference: `#E28E56`, adjusted only if later sampling confirms a closer logo colour
- Canvas: `#F3F2EF`
- Steel grey: `#687078`
- Functional blue: active workflow
- Functional amber: waiting or pending
- Functional green: approved, completed, or paid
- Functional red: urgent, critical, or overdue

## Typography
Use Geist Sans for interface typography and Geist Mono only for IDs, registration numbers, VINs, and compact operational codes. Use strong hierarchy, readable sizes, and restrained uppercase labels.

## Logo Rules
- Authoritative file: `/public/brand/jb-motors-logo.png`
- Preserve the file unchanged and render it at its original aspect ratio.
- Do not redraw, generate, recolour, crop, stretch, remove its background, or separate its tyre mark and wordmark.
- On dark navigation, place the full logo inside a deliberate white or warm-neutral brand rail.
- Use the same asset on estimates, invoices, settings, technician, and customer experiences.

## Design Do Not Do
- No generic blue SaaS dashboard identity
- No glassmorphism or glossy gradient surfaces
- No oversized rounded cards or excessive pill shapes
- No invented automotive clip-art or AI-generated dashboard imagery
- No fake futuristic telemetry or AI features
- No decorative icons without operational meaning
- No long screens made entirely from identical cards

## Anti-Generic Test
If the JB Motors logo were temporarily hidden, the compact workshop flow, service-mileage emphasis, vehicle history, job-card depth, and human follow-up centre must still identify the product as an automotive workshop operating system.

# Business Case

## Business Problem
JB Motors needs one reliable operational picture of customers, vehicles, estimates, jobs, parts, invoices, payments, and follow-ups instead of fragmented paper records and messages.

## Why It Matters
Poor workflow visibility delays vehicles, hides outstanding approvals and balances, weakens service history, and allows service-retention opportunities to be missed.

## Target Outcome
Give Jabulani and workshop staff a practical daily operating interface that makes the next action obvious and helps JB Motors maintain customer relationships after each vehicle leaves.

## Value Proposition
JB Motors professionally manages each vehicle from arrival to its next service through connected workshop workflow, history, and human follow-up.

## Commercial Model
Internal productivity and customer-retention platform commissioned for one JB Motors workshop.

## Revenue Logic
Improve conversion from estimate to job, reduce missed follow-ups, surface outstanding invoices, control parts usage, and encourage timely repeat servicing.

## Risks to Commercial Success
- Staff may reject a tool that is slower than existing habits.
- Unverified contact or commercial details could undermine owner trust.
- A visually generic interface could look like a white-label ERP rather than a JB Motors product.
- Building backend complexity before owner validation could waste budget.

## Minimum Proof Needed
Jabulani can complete a realistic customer-to-payment walkthrough, a technician can update a job on mobile, and a customer can submit kilometres or a request without confusion.

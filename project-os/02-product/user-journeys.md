# User Journeys

## Workshop lifecycle
Customer and vehicle are found or created, a complaint or request is reviewed, an estimate is prepared and approved, work is converted to a job card, labour and parts are recorded, QC and road-test checks are completed, an invoice and payment are recorded, and the vehicle history becomes available for future service follow-up.

## Customer retention
Thabo opens the mobile portal, sees what the Toyota Corolla needs next, updates the odometer, requests a service or reports a symptom, receives an honest acknowledgement, and waits for a human confirmation from JB Motors.

## Technician
The Technician role opens assigned jobs, reviews complaint and inspection details, adds notes or a photo control, records parts, moves work through status, and completes QC without seeing finance or administrative settings.

## Owner / Administrator
Jabulani reviews the attention centre, workload, follow-ups and revenue context; switches into customer, vehicle, job, invoice and inventory records; and controls confirmed workshop settings. Unconfirmed business facts remain clearly pending.

## Failure and empty paths
Loading, empty and error states preserve context and provide a safe return to demo records. Mock submissions never claim persistence, instant booking, payment processing or automated customer contact.

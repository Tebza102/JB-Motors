# Out of Scope

## Not building in this phase
- Firebase, databases, authentication, real roles or persistence
- Real file/media uploads, PDF generation, payments, email, SMS or WhatsApp APIs
- External source upload, hosting or deployment
- GPS or telematics mileage collection
- AI-assisted troubleshooting
- Multi-workshop, franchise or multi-tenant SaaS controls

## Reason
This phase is an owner-reviewable local frontend prototype. Production data ownership, security, POPIA controls, operational rules and integration accounts require separate decisions.

## Parking lot
| Capability | Revisit when |
|---|---|
| Firebase and RBAC | Prototype and data model are approved |
| Real notifications | Contact details, consent and provider are confirmed |
| GPS / telematics source | Hardware/provider and consent model are selected |
| AI troubleshooting | Qualified-inspection guardrails and data sources are approved |

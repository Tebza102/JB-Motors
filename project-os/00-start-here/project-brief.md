# Project Brief

## Project Name
JB Motors Digital Workshop Management Platform

## Project Type
Single-workshop web application, internal operations tool, customer portal, and mobile technician interface.

## Business Purpose
Give JB Motors one practical digital operating system for managing the customer and vehicle lifecycle from first request through service history and future follow-up. The platform is owned by JB Motors and is not a multi-tenant SaaS or franchise product.

## Business Identity
- Business: JB Motors
- Owner / Administrator: Jabulani
- Location: Ratanda, Heidelberg, Gauteng (GP)
- Telephone, WhatsApp, email, street address, VAT number, labour rate, and commercial rules: pending owner confirmation

## Primary Users
- Jabulani as Owner / Administrator
- Service Advisor / Front Desk
- Technician
- Store / Parts Clerk
- Finance / Accounts User
- JB Motors customers using the mobile portal

## Core Problem
Workshop information is easily fragmented across calls, messages, paper job cards, invoices, parts records, and personal memory. JB Motors needs clear workflow status, accountable vehicle history, and a practical human follow-up queue.

## MVP Goal
Deliver a complete clickable, mobile-first frontend prototype covering workshop operations, technician work, and customer self-service with realistic South African demo data. No production backend, authentication, persistence, uploads, or external integrations are active in this phase.

## Success Metrics
- Every requested navigation item opens a purposeful screen.
- The estimate-to-job-to-invoice lifecycle is demonstrable.
- Customer kilometre, service-request, and problem-report flows reach confirmation states.
- Role switching limits unnecessary navigation.
- Desktop, tablet, staff-mobile, and customer-mobile layouts remain usable.
- Lint and production build pass from the canonical local project path.

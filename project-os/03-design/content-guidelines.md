# Content Guidelines

## Voice and Tone
Professional, direct, calm, technically competent, and helpful. Use plain South African business English. Customers describe symptoms in their own words; JB Motors owns technical diagnosis.

## Writing Rules
- Say what needs attention and what action comes next.
- Use Rands, kilometres, South African registration formats, and clear local dates.
- Keep sample operational records realistic but clearly part of a prototype.
- Use Jabulani only where the Owner / Administrator identity is required.
- Use Ratanda, Heidelberg, Gauteng for the confirmed business location.
- Do not invent phone, WhatsApp, email, street address, VAT, registration, labour-rate, or commercial-policy facts.

## CTA Rules
Use direct outcomes: `Call customer`, `Review report`, `Open job`, `Convert to job card`, `Record payment`, `Request service`, `Update kilometres`, and `Report a problem`.

## Customer Safety Rules
- Do not ask customers to diagnose mechanical causes.
- Do not imply instant booking.
- Tell customers to stop safely and contact the workshop when a vehicle may be unsafe.
- Do not imply automated messages were sent when the prototype only changes local state.

## State Copy
- Loading: explain what record is being prepared.
- Empty: explain why the list is empty and give the next relevant action.
- Error: explain that prototype data could not be shown and provide a retry/reset action.
- Success: confirm the completed local interaction and state what JB Motors will do next only where the workflow supports human follow-up.

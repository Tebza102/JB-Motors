# Target User

## Primary User
Jabulani, the JB Motors Owner / Administrator, who needs immediate visibility of workshop workload, customer needs, money owed, and operational bottlenecks.

## Secondary Users
- Service Advisor / Front Desk
- Technician
- Store / Parts Clerk
- Finance / Accounts User
- JB Motors customers using mobile self-service

## User Pain Points
- Job status and workshop priorities are difficult to see at a glance.
- Customer, vehicle, service, parts, and payment history can become disconnected.
- Service reminders and reported problems require disciplined human follow-up.
- Mobile workshop users need fast updates with minimal typing.

## User Jobs To Be Done
- Receive and understand a customer request.
- Track a vehicle through estimate, approval, work, QC, invoice, and payment.
- Find accurate customer and vehicle history.
- Know who needs a call or message today.
- Let customers update kilometres and describe symptoms without self-diagnosing.

## User Constraints
Limited time, mixed technical confidence, mobile workshop conditions, practical South African SMME workflows, and a need to confirm business rules before production use.

## Buying Trigger
The workshop needs to professionalise daily operations and customer retention without adopting a complex multi-branch ERP.

## Adoption Risks
Too much data entry, cluttered screens, unreliable mobile layouts, generic styling, false automation, or unclear ownership of updates.

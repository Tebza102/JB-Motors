# MVP Scope

## MVP Objective
Prove that one coherent JB Motors interface can support the complete workshop lifecycle and customer-retention experience before backend investment.

## Included in MVP
- Complete clickable workshop dashboard and role-aware navigation
- Customer directory and relationship detail
- Vehicle directory, vehicle profile, service history, and structured kilometre history
- Estimate list/detail and conversion demonstration
- Job-card table, board, detail, technician notes, parts, QC, and status flow
- Inventory, supplier, stock-movement, invoice, payment, warranty, request, follow-up, report, and settings interfaces
- Mobile technician interface
- Mobile customer vehicle, service request, problem report, kilometre update, history, and profile flows
- Loading, empty, error, success, and confirmation states
- Responsive desktop, tablet, staff-mobile, and customer-mobile layouts

## Excluded from MVP
- Authentication and real role enforcement
- Database persistence and server APIs
- Real file/photo/video uploads
- Payment processing, email, SMS, WhatsApp Business, GPS, telematics, and AI troubleshooting
- Multi-branch, franchise, or multi-tenant administration
- Public deployment

## MVP User Flow
Customer or front desk creates the initial need → vehicle is assessed → estimate is reviewed → approved work becomes a job card → labour and parts are recorded → QC marks the vehicle ready → invoice and payment position are shown → service history and follow-up become visible.

## MVP Demo Script
1. Open the workshop dashboard and inspect Needs Attention.
2. Open Thabo Mokoena’s Toyota Corolla and review service and kilometre history.
3. Review an approved estimate and convert it to a job card.
4. Move the job through the workshop board and technician workflow.
5. Review the invoice and payment balance.
6. Open Customer Follow-ups and record a human contact action.
7. Switch to the customer portal, update kilometres, request service, and submit a problem report.

## MVP Completion Checklist
- All planned screens are purposeful and navigable.
- Client-side interactions and states work.
- UI is usable on desktop and mobile.
- Lint and production build pass.
- Exact logo, owner, and location are correct.
- Prototype and local-only status are documented.
